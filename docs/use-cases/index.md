# Use cases

What home-feeling must do, and what its users see when it works. Identifiers are stable: a use case is never renumbered.

## Actors

- **Desktop user**: runs the app and uses its Things.
- **Thing author**: writes or edits a Thing's Lua files. Often the same person as the desktop user.
- **Maintainer**: builds and publishes releases.

## Areas

- [Things](things.md): UC-THINGS-01 to 05. Listing, reloading, diagnosing, approving, and configuring Things.
- [Tiling](tiling.md): UC-TILE-01 to 05. The tiling window manager Thing, Windows only.
- [Install, update, and release](install.md): UC-INSTALL-01, 02, UC-UPDATE-01, UC-RELEASE-01.

## Not in v1

- Workspaces.
- Window rules by class, executable, or title.
- Layouts other than dwindle.
- Tiling and overlays on Linux, X11 or Wayland.
- Distributing or discovering third-party Things.
